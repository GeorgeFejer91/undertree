# wrAIter for your office suite

<img src="../assets/wraiter/icon.svg" width="80" height="80" alt="wrAIter AI waiter and typewriter logo">

Choose the package for your existing office suite. Each add-on has its own
connection and runs independently; UnderTree is not required.

| Host | Package | Build and installation instructions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| LibreOffice Writer 7.6 or later | `wrAIter.oxt` | [Writer add-on](../modules/libreoffice/README.md#install) |
| Microsoft Word desktop on Windows | `wrAIter-Word_0.1.0_windows-setup.exe` | [Word add-in](../modules/word/README.md) |

Public downloads, when available, are listed in the repository's
[GitHub releases](https://github.com/GeorgeFejer91/undertree/releases). A local
build is not a published release. Follow the package-specific platform and
qualification notes; the existing UnderTree desktop installers are separate.
Save your work and close the relevant host before upgrading its add-on.

The approved mark identifies both packages, their primary ribbon/toolbar entry
and native add-on windows. Highlight colors and action icons retain their own
meanings; seeing the logo does not indicate AI access or document sharing.
